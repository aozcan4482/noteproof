Mining and Consensus
Mining is an important part of the Bitcoin network. Miners help confirm transactions and add new blocks to the blockchain.

Miners try to find a block hash that is lower than a specific target. They change a number called nonce and calculate the hash again and again until they find a valid result.

This process is called Proof-of-Work. It requires computer power and many attempts. When a miner finds a valid block, they share it with the network.

The miner receives a reward for finding a block. This reward includes newly created Bitcoin and the transaction fees from the transactions inside the block.

Bitcoin also has a difficulty adjustment system. The difficulty changes every 2016 blocks to keep the average block time around 10 minutes.

Consensus means that the nodes in the network agree on which blockchain is valid. They follow the valid chain with the most accumulated work.

The main idea is that mining helps protect the network and makes it difficult to change the transaction history.
